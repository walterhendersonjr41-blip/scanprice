ScanPrice

Point your phone camera at a room. Every few seconds the app snaps a frame, an AI vision model names up to 8 objects it sees and estimates a realistic US market price for each, and price tags float over the live picture.

Demo page. The real app runs on Base44 (login + AI vision). This GitHub Pages copy has no AI and sends nothing anywhere: the camera picture stays on your device and the price tags are simulated sample prices, not real estimates.

How the real app works

  • Full-screen camera view with a 5-second countdown between scans and a scan counter.
  • Each scan uploads one frame and asks the AI for JSON: up to 8 items with a short name, a USD price and the item's position (% of width/height).
  • Animated price tags are placed over each item; a new scan replaces them.
  • Built with Base44 (React + Tailwind, Base44 auth, Core.UploadFile and Core.InvokeLLM integrations).

The production build is a compiled Base44 bundle tied to Base44's backend, so it isn't included; this page shows the idea.
